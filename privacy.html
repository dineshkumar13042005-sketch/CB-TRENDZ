1. Introduction

CB TRENDZ ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to sell sarees and ladies' fashion and accessories across India. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By using our website or placing an order, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to process your orders and serve you better:

  • Identity & contact details: name, mobile number, email address.
  • Delivery details: full address, city, state and pincode.
  • Order details: products purchased, amounts, order and payment status.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information and pages visited, to keep the site working properly.
  • WhatsApp communication: messages and details you share when you order or contact us on WhatsApp.
We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking credentials. Payments are handled by our payment partners.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, confirm, pack, ship and deliver your orders.
  • To contact you about order status, delivery, returns or support.
  • To verify payments and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To send offers, new arrivals and updates, only if you have subscribed (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To improve our website, products and service.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. Payments & Third-Party Services

Online payments are processed securely by third-party payment providers such as Cashfree Payments, Razorpay, and UPI apps like PhonePe and Google Pay. When you pay, you are subject to their own terms and privacy policies. We receive only the payment status and reference needed to confirm your order.

We also share your name, phone number and delivery address with courier and logistics partners strictly to deliver your order. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember your shopping bag and wishlist on your device. This data stays in your browser and is not used to track you across other sites. Third-party services (such as payment gateways and Google Fonts) may set their own cookies. You can clear local storage or cookies anytime from your browser settings; doing so will empty your bag and wishlist.

6. Data Retention

We keep order and billing records for as long as required for accounting, tax and legal purposes, and keep other information only as long as needed for the purposes described above. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including secure server-side payment verification, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights & Choices

In line with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information (subject to records we must legally retain).
  • Withdraw consent to marketing messages at any time.

To exercise these rights, write to us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. External Links

Our website may link to third-party sites such as Instagram or WhatsApp. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices; please read their policies separately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.
