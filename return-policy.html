1. Overview & Commitment

At CB TRENDZ, we take pride in curating authentic, hand-inspected sarees including Kanchi cotton, semi-silk, linen, georgette, and designer weaves. Every parcel is thoroughly checked for weaving defects, tears, and quality before dispatch. We strive to make your shopping experience smooth, trustworthy, and satisfying.

2. Return Eligibility

A return or replacement request is eligible under the following circumstances:

  • You received a damaged, torn, or defective saree.
  • You received the wrong product or wrong color variant compared to your confirmed order.
  • The request is initiated within 48 hours of parcel delivery.
  • The saree must remain in its original, brand-new condition: unused, unworn, unwashed, with no alterations, and with original folds, tags, and packaging intact.

3. Mandatory Parcel Unboxing Video

Important Requirement: To ensure fairness and protect both customers and our boutique, a clear, continuous, uncut parcel unboxing video is mandatory for any return or damage claim.

The video must show:

  • The outer package seal and shipping label clearly visible before opening.
  • Continuous opening of the courier pouch without any cuts, pauses, or edits.
  • Careful unfolding of the saree showing the specific defect or damage clearly.

4. How to Request a Return

  1. Record your unboxing video upon receiving the package.
  2. Contact our customer care team via WhatsApp at 8778522690 or email cbtrendzofficial@gmail.com within 48 hours.
  3. Share your Order ID, unboxing video, and clear photos of the issue.
  4. Our team will inspect the details and confirm the return approval within 24 business hours.

5. Non-Returnable Scenarios

Returns cannot be accepted in the following cases:

  • Requests raised after 48 hours of parcel delivery.
  • Claims without a continuous parcel unboxing video.
  • Sarees that have been worn, washed, perfume-sprayed, ironed, or modified.
  • Slight color tone differences (5–10%) resulting from digital screen calibrations, monitor brightness, and photography studio lighting.
  • Minor handloom thread slubs or slight irregularities in zari borders, which are natural hallmarks of authentic traditional handlooms rather than manufacturing defects.
  • Change of mind after safe delivery.

6. Exchange & Replacement

Upon return approval, we will gladly arrange a free replacement of the same saree model (subject to stock availability). If the exact product is out of stock, you may choose an alternative saree of equivalent value or request a full monetary refund.

7. Refund Timeline & Method

  • Once the returned saree reaches our facility and passes physical inspection, your refund will be initiated within 24 to 48 hours.
  • For UPI payments (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, QR code), the refund is credited directly to your bank account / UPI ID within 2 to 4 business days.
  • For payments via Cashfree / Razorpay gateway (Cards & NetBanking), refunds typically reflect within 5 to 7 business days as per banking network standards.

8. Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before parcel dispatch by contacting our WhatsApp support team at 8778522690. Once the courier tracking number has been generated and the package dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible.
